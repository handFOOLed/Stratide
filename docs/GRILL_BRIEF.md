# Grill-with-docs — Stratide architecture interrogation

## Brief for Codex

Use the `grill-with-docs` skill against this repository. Read `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/RISK_POLICY.md`, and `docs/M0.md` first. Challenge meaningful ambiguities with **one tightly scoped question at a time**, concrete scenarios, recommended answer with rationale, and alternatives. Do not ask the owner to re-answer settled product preferences. Record decisions as a concise glossary entry or ADR only when they actually crystallize; avoid boilerplate documentation.

Use this opening frame:

> We are designing a low-resource local-first trading engine paired with cloud-only autonomous research. Explore trading strategies without preset strategy-family restrictions; never allow research/AI to bypass financial risk controls. The owner wants practical, risk-proportional engineering, not exhaustive hypothetical edge-case testing. Challenge the riskiest assumptions before writing implementation code.

## Already decided (do not re-litigate)

- Open-ended autonomous strategy research (including generated strategy code).
- Cloud-only LLM inference, no production GPU requirement.
- Paper testing and local Windows before any live money.
- Independent risk authority and loss circuit breakers.
- Low operating cost, low local resource requirements.
- Future macOS ARM64 Mac mini or Linux mini PC and private Web Dashboard only after evidence of value.
- **ADR-001 accepted:** hybrid local/remote strategy computation with unified proposals, always locally risk-gated. Do not re-litigate hybrid vs all-local vs all-cloud.
- **ADR-002 accepted:** Risk Kernel and Execution Engine are separate modules inside one trusted Rust core process; one mandatory order gateway; untrusted strategy code stays outside it. Do not re-litigate the basic in-process vs separate-daemon choice without concrete financial risk evidence.
- **ADR-003 accepted:** uncertain broker submission freezes all new risk for the account, not only the affected ticker; preserve state, reconcile orders/fills/cash/positions and never blindly retry.
- **ADR-004 accepted:** recovery of technical-only broker halts is configurable (`conditional_auto` default / `manual`), but always needs full reconciliation and stable connection; repeated faults require Owner intervention. Loss/drawdown breakers never auto-clear as a side effect.
- **ADR-005 accepted:** incomplete/low-cost historical data may be used for exploratory research, but promotion-grade historical backtests need independently verified point-in-time inputs; forward-observed shadow trading can build new evidence when historical availability is unprovable. Do not equate shadow testing with live deployment.
- **ADR-006 accepted:** Agent freely explores, but cannot inspect/alter protected evaluation rules or repeatedly use the same holdout as an unlimited tuning oracle. Freeze and hash candidates, track attempts and research lineage, use genuine forward observation before live eligibility. Do not mandate complex statistics or arbitrary test-count limits at M0.
- **ADR-007 accepted:** initial paper/backtesting fills use conservative daily-bar assumptions, with instrument-specific tradability, fees and timing; no assumed favorable fills from OHLC alone. Higher-fidelity market simulation only when the strategy/data actually requires it.
- **ADR-008 accepted:** portfolio allocation may change weights among eligible strategies, but every formal backtest/forward-paper run must start with at most the Owner-authorized account principal. Combined multi-strategy paper execution must share one cash ledger and cannot double-count capital; default no borrowing or leverage. Allocation does not itself authorize live promotion.
- **ADR-009 accepted:** develop toward B (automatic, independently qualified strategy promotion within separately Owner-enabled live capital and risk limits), while Owner can reject or disable any strategy for any reason. A trusted Promotion Gate applies vetoes; Agent cannot approve itself, override a veto, or expand limits. Do NOT quietly reintroduce mandatory per-strategy approval, and do NOT enable real orders in M0.
- **ADR-010 superseded by ADR-011:** default grace period is now 0m, with a 10m delay only if the Owner opts in. Ordinary notifications must not delay qualified autonomous promotion; avoid nightly/manual strategy review queues. Independent qualification and portfolio/risk gating still apply, and risk controls are not a guarantee of capped losses.
- **ADR-012 accepted:** Agent may adaptively prioritize research experiments but may never raise the Owner-set daily research hard cap. A trusted budget gate reserves cost before paid jobs, handles concurrency and billing uncertainty; research exhaustion does not stop existing trading/risk operations. Exact amount and reset timezone are Owner-configured later.
- **ADR-013 accepted:** Owner gives a single persisted authorization for each material cloud service/purpose/data scope and is not repeatedly prompted for ordinary subsequent research requests. Owner can authorize rich account/transaction context and revoke future sharing. No broker credentials, execution permissions or risk-config write access ever leave trusted local control. New receiver/category/purpose requires new consent; actual provider retention/data licenses still need checking.
- **ADR-014 accepted:** generated strategies execute in separate restricted local or isolated remote Workers, with flexible Python/scientific/ML dependencies. Only authenticated, versioned, expiring trade proposals reach the trusted Rust core; no Worker broker/ledger/risk-write access. WASM is optional, not mandatory. Process isolation alone is not a sandbox; actual filesystem/network/resource restrictions need engineering when Workers are implemented.
- **ADR-015 accepted:** research Agent may automatically discover and vet public data sources; trusted Data Manager handles production adapters, source provenance, credentials, licensing, quotas and quality. Production strategy Workers have no arbitrary internet access. Built-in free compliant sources should require no repeated user setup; new secrets/payments/sensitive data sharing require initial Owner authorization. Data Manager is a logical boundary, not a new required service.
- **ADR-016 accepted:** automatically use verified fresh backup data sources after primary failure; normalize time, security IDs and price semantics first. Material unexplained disagreement or no valid feed blocks new risk for affected strategies. ADR-003 broker order uncertainty remains an account-wide halt. No Owner review for routine failover.
- **ADR-017 accepted:** one trading writer per account; same-host exclusion, durable order/ledger/breaker/veto state and broker reconciliation precede new live orders after restart. No dual-active or automatic backup takeover. Cross-host move requires retiring the previous host's order authority; local file locks are not distributed locks. M0 tests narrow paper-only restart consistency, not disaster recovery systems.
- **ADR-018 accepted:** daily-loss and peak-to-trough drawdown breakers use trusted cash-flow-adjusted whole-account equity, including unrealized P&L. Owner deposits/withdrawals are not gains/losses. Materially unknown NAV cannot be used to bypass the account breaker; persist financial halts, block new account risk and never reset on technical recovery or midnight. Threshold percentages remain Owner-unconfigured; M0 only needs a small paper test.
- **ADR-019 accepted:** dual Risk Kernel constraints: Owner account-wide cash/exposure/NAV locks apply to all strategies, while a losing strategy's own budget/loss lock stops only that strategy's new risk. Attribution and pending-order reservations must avoid double-counting a shared cash/position pool. Persist strategy lineage and blocks; no rename/clone evasion. Specific strategy thresholds and financial-lock reinstatement are not decided.
- **ADR-020 accepted:** changed strategy versions require independent requalification before automatic promotion; prior version losses and locks persist.
- **ADR-021 accepted:** each strategy group's research and internal signals are isolated, even when groups hold the same ticker; real account cash, positions and limits are shared.
- **ADR-022 accepted:** preserve independently generated per-group intents, then let trusted Portfolio Allocator aggregate compatible opposing intents into one net external order before risk/execution. Zero external orders may be appropriate if auditable eligible virtual position reallocation exists; never fabricate exchange fills or bypass sellable inventory/T+1. ADR-023 selects a common market-reference price; exact quote field and partial fill assignment remain open.
- **ADR-023 accepted:** trusted Portfolio Allocator uses the same timely validated market-reference price for both groups in a virtual share-allocation transfer. No bid/ask spread or real broker fees may be invented for an internal reassignment. Preserve group attribution while total real cash, shares and NAV remain unchanged; exact quote field and partial-fill allocation remain open.
- **ADR-024 accepted:** combined broker orders' confirmed partial fills are attributed pro rata among eligible groups at the same prior committed priority. Trusted genuinely risk-reducing intents may receive earlier priority; Agent cannot self-declare it. Deterministic integer rounding, fees/price attribution, replay-safe fill IDs and exact broker-to-group totals are mandatory. Detailed remainder and price-lot algorithm is deferred until real multi-group execution.
- **ADR-025 accepted:** Strategy admission and risk budgeting are distinct. Use small initial budgets, slow evidence-based expansion, faster **restriction of new risk** on verified deterioration, and account/strategy Risk Kernel enforcement. Volatile markets are not proof of alpha death. The budget planner must be deterministic/auditable, bounded, anti-churn, fail-closed on stale data, tested by replay, stress/fault injection and prolonged paper/shadow evidence before any Owner-authorized live feature enablement. No complex decay model or allocator in M0.
- **ADR-026 accepted:** a limited shadow-observation pool with explicit CPU, storage and research budgets; pause/archive less useful strategies with honest observation gaps. A local SQLite Strategy Registry filters authorized metadata and returns bounded summaries before cloud LLM calls. Keep group-private research isolated and preserve essential financial/qualification evidence. No research search/vector systems in M0.
- **ADR-027 accepted:** past failed strategy evaluations are reusable evidence, not a permanent ban on new research. The local authorized registry provides bounded failure context and dedupe signals; Agent should identify a testable changed hypothesis before costly re-research. Old loss locks, Owner veto, protected holdouts and trusted independent new-version promotion still bind; M0 has no failure-research engine.
- **ADR-028 accepted:** Initial real-money use requires a brokerage account dedicated to Stratide, distinct from the Owner's ordinary manual trades. Internal strategy groups still share one real account ledger. Do not infer account exclusivity means skipping broker reconciliation; unexplained external fills/balance/holdings must freeze new account risk and be reconciled. Exact emergency manual takeover and actual broker account/API feasibility remain to decide. No live account in M0.
- **ADR-029 accepted:** explicit persisted Owner manual-takeover lock disables all new Stratide broker submissions. It survives restart/network recovery, and even ADR-004 conditional technical recovery cannot release it. Only authenticated Owner return plus authoritative broker reconciliation and all independent risk checks can reinstate automatic execution. Handling of preexisting broker orders is still open. M0 has no handoff UI.
- Financial-risk-driven testing; do not spend days on harmless malformed names.

## Decision tree — highest-value unresolved choices

1. **ADR-014/015/016 implementation (open):** minimal trusted Worker/Data Manager contract, real OS isolation and source failover logic when approved providers exist. Specific data freshness/conflict thresholds remain open.
2. **Risk gateway / ADR-018/019 implementation (open):** mandatory account and strategy constraints; cash-flow-adjusted NAV, attribution and persistent lock reason; future Owner limits and strategy requalification rule. Do not confuse financial locks with ADR-004 technical auto recovery.
3. **ADR-003/004 implementation (open):** minimal order identifiers, broker reconciliation and bounded query behavior; exact stability/repeated-fault policy should be tuned for the actual adapter, not speculated about now.
4. **ADR-005 implementation (open):** minimal point-in-time evidence metadata, forward-observation journaling and corporate-action handling; no exhaustive data-quality engineering at M0.
5. **ADR-006 implementation (open):** choose minimal candidate freeze, evaluator feedback, held-out data isolation and eventually appropriate thresholds; core principle is settled.
6. **ADR-007 implementation (open):** when instruments/data are chosen, define a minimal conservative daily-bar fill model, fee schedule and supported tradability rules; avoid inventing a tick-level simulator.
7. **ADR-009/011 implementation (open):** minimal trusted immediate Promotion Gate, optional Owner-configured grace period, durable veto, nonblocking low-noise notifications and proper incident alerts; separate initial live authorization remains required.
8. **ADR-017 implementation (open):** smallest safe single-writer recovery mechanism, transaction-consistent SQLite backup/restore and controlled live authority handoff to a supported Mac mini/Linux host; no automated hot standby.
9. **ADR-013 implementation (open):** straightforward one-time consent UI and trusted durable scope enforcement, plus real vendor retention/training and third-party market-data licensing checks. No repetitive field-level popups.
10. **ADR-012 implementation (open):** choose smallest trusted daily research-cost ledger, conservative reservation model and provider budget alerts when paid agents are introduced; do not guess the Owner's daily monetary limit or timezone.
